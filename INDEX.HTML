Stay Safe: Prevention Tips

Simple habits that protect you from common illnesses

🧼

Wash Your Hands

Regular handwashing with soap reduces the spread of infections like flu and diarrhea.

💉

Stay Vaccinated

Keep up to date with vaccines — they remain one of the most effective ways to prevent disease.

🥗

Eat Balanced Meals

A diet rich in fruits, vegetables, and clean water strengthens your immune system.

🩺

Regular Checkups

Catching health issues early makes them far easier and cheaper to treat.

"Your health is not just the absence of illness — it's the presence of care, awareness, and prevention."
